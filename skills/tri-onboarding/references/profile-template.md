# Profile template

Write `athlete/profile.md` with these sections, in the athlete's language. Keep every section, write "unknown" where needed, and give the source of each figure in brackets: (said), (to find), (to measure), (estimated).

```markdown

Updated: <date>

- Start time and cut-off times:
- Swim: <pool, lake, sea, river>, wetsuit <yes, no, unknown>
- Bike: <distance>, <elevation gain> (source: <organiser, past report, unknown>), notable climbs:
- Run: <distance>, <profile>
- Goal: <finish, or a time to confirm after the first tests>
- Weeks to race: <n> (minimum for this format: <n>) <warning if under>

## Real week
- Slots really free, day by day:
- Places:
- Pool and real opening hours:
- Riding: <outdoor roads, home trainer>
- Recurring travel:
- Hours ceiling per week:
- Last complete, normal week (<date>): swim <h>, bike <h>, run <h>, total <h>
- Longest recent ride: <min>. Longest recent run: <min>. Continuous swim: <m>

## Equipment level
- Indoor bike: level <1, 2, 3>
- Outdoor bike: level <1, 2, 3> (power meter on the bike: <yes, no>)
- Run: level <1, 2, 3>
- Swim: level <1, 3>
- Bike and positions: <type>, <hoods, drops, aero bars>, aero bars can be fitted: <yes, no, unknown>

- Recovery signal and thresholds chosen by the athlete:

## Level of detail

## Unknowns to resolve
- <what>, <who finds it>, <how>
```
