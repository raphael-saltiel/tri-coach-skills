# Onboarding questions

For each block: what to ask, the options to offer, and what goes wrong when the answer is missing. Ask in the athlete's language.

## 1. The race and its real profile

| Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Which race, on what date? Format: sprint, olympic, 70.3 or Ironman? | Everything is counted back from the race date: phases, ramp, taper. |
| What time is the start? Are there cut-off times (swim, bike, finish)? | A late start can mean finishing near dusk. The race-day estimate checks the margin on each cut-off. |
| Swim: pool, lake, sea or river? Wetsuit allowed, compulsory, forbidden? | Open water is slower than the pool and needs practice sessions, never alone. |
| Bike: what is the real elevation gain? Any steep climb? | A hilly course can cost a beginner an hour or more over a flat one. A time model built on an assumed flat course is wrong. |
| Run: flat, hilly, how many laps? | Changes the long-run terrain and the race-day pace. |
| Goal: to finish, or a time? | A time goal is only set after the first tests. Until then the goal is to finish. |

If the athlete has not chosen a race yet, stop here and point to tri-guide (choosing a first race).

Where to find the course profile: the organiser's page, the official route file, a race report from a past edition. Write the source in the profile.

## 2. The real week

| Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Which days and time slots are really free? Morning, lunch, evening? | Sessions are placed in real slots, not ideal ones. |
| Where are you on each day? Is there a second place at weekends? | A session needs its place: the pool near work, the roads near the weekend house. |
| Which pool, and what are its real opening hours for lane swimming? | Pool hours are the hardest constraint of the week. Ask the athlete to check them. |
| Where do you ride: road, bike path, home trainer? | Decides which rides can be outdoors and what the bad-weather fallback is. |
| Any recurring travel or periods without a bike or a pool? | Each one needs a planned fallback, not an improvised one. |
| What is the most you can train in a week, honestly? | It becomes the ceiling of the plan. |
| What did you really do in your last complete, normal week, sport by sport? | It becomes week 1 of the ramp. An average that includes a break starts the ramp too low, and a wish starts it too high. |
| Your longest ride and longest run in the last month? | Starting points of the long sessions. |
| How far do you swim without stopping? | Feeds the base gate for the 1000 m test. |

## 3. Equipment

| Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Do you have a heart-rate sensor (watch or strap)? | Level 2. |
| A home trainer that shows watts? A power meter on the bike itself? | Level 3 indoors only, or outdoors too. Outdoor sessions must never be written in watts without a power meter. |
| A GPS watch for running pace? A pool watch or just the wall clock? | Pace steering. |
| What bike, and which positions can you hold: hoods, drops, aero bars? Can aero bars be fitted on your handlebar? | The position drives the bike estimate. Some handlebars cannot take aero bars: ask before advising any. |
| Which devices and apps record your sessions? | Used by tri-connect to pick the data route. |

## 4. Body data

Ask for height. Then: "Weight and bike weight are optional. They are only used to estimate your bike time on the real course. Do you want to give them?" Accept a refusal without comment and move on.

## 5. Health and injury history

| Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Any injury in the last two years? Where, when, is it resolved? | Shapes the strength work and makes the ramp more careful for that sport. |
| Any pain right now, even small? | Pain on two sessions in a row removes that sport for the week. Better to know before week 1. |
| Any medical condition or treatment I should know about? | Out of scope: note the constraint as told, recommend the doctor, give no advice on it. |
| When was your last medical check? Has a doctor cleared you for intense effort? | Feeds the health gate before any maximal test. |

## 6. Preferences

| Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Morning or evening? How many sessions a week do you want? | Two sessions a day only come when single sessions are full. |
| Anything you refuse: gym, trainer, cold water? | A plan that ignores a refusal is not followed. |
| Other sports you want to keep? | Counted in the week as easy load. |
| How do you judge recovery: a device score, or how you feel in the morning? Do you have your own thresholds? | The recovery signal has three levels. The thresholds are the athlete's choice, not a norm. |
| How do you want feedback: short and blunt, or detailed? | The default is short, direct, with numbers. |
