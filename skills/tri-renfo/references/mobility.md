# Mobility, 10 minutes

A basic routine of this pack: gentle, without equipment, for the four areas triathlon tightens. It is general maintenance, not a treatment. Move into each position slowly, stay where the stretch is clear and comfortable, breathe out slowly. No bouncing, no pain.

| Area | Movement | How | Dose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hips, front | Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch | One knee on the floor, the other foot forward. Tuck the pelvis under and shift the hips forward. | 2 x 40 s each side |
| Hips, back | Figure-four stretch | On the back, one ankle on the opposite knee. Pull the legs towards the chest. | 2 x 40 s each side |
| Ankles and calves | Knee-to-wall | Foot a hand's width from a wall. Drive the knee to the wall, heel down. | 2 x 10 slow reps each side |
| Upper back | Open book | On the side, knees bent, arms together in front. Open the top arm to the other side, following it with the eyes. | 2 x 8 each side |
| Shoulders | Wall slide | Back and forearms against a wall. Slide the arms up and down, keeping contact. | 2 x 10 |

When: after a session, or in the evening. It can follow the core routine. On the day before a test, mobility is fine: it is not strength work.

If a movement gives a sharp or pinching pain, stop that movement and note it for the journal. No diagnosis is given here.
