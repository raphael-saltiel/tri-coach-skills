# Routines and exercises

Rest 30 seconds between sets unless stated. Breathe normally: never hold the breath. A set stops when the form goes.

## Core, 12 minutes

| Exercise | How | Form cue | Easier version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plank | On the forearms and toes, body in one line | Hips neither sagging nor raised. Squeeze the glutes. | On the knees |
| Side plank | On one forearm and the side of the foot, body in one line | Hips forward and high, the top shoulder stacked over the bottom one | Bottom knee on the floor |
| Single-leg glute bridge | On the back, one foot on the floor, the other leg up. Lift the hips. | Push through the heel. Hips level, no twist. One second at the top. | Both feet on the floor |
| Superman | Face down, arms forward. Lift arms, chest and legs slightly. | Look at the floor, long neck. Lift low and long, not high. | Lift one arm and the opposite leg, alternating |

Order: plank, side plank left and right, glute bridge left and right, superman. That is one round. Three rounds, or the three sets of each exercise in a row.

| | Load week | Recovery week |
|---|---|---|
| Plank | 3 x 60 s | 3 x 45 s |
| Side plank, each side | 3 x 40 s | 3 x 30 s |
| Single-leg glute bridge, each side | 3 x 15 | 3 x 12 |
| Superman | 3 x 40 s | 3 x 30 s |

## Preventive strength, 20 minutes

| Exercise | How | Form cue | Easier version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calf raise, knee straight | Standing on one leg, on the edge of a step or on the floor, knee straight. Rise onto the toes, come down slowly. | Two seconds up, three seconds down. A wall or a chair for balance only. | On both legs |
| Single-leg glute bridge | As above | As above | Both feet on the floor |
| Reverse lunge | Standing. Step one foot back and lower the back knee towards the floor, then come back up. | Front knee over the foot, chest tall. Push through the front heel. | Shorter step, shallower, a hand on a chair |
| Side plank | As above | As above | Bottom knee on the floor |

| | Load week | Recovery week |
|---|---|---|
| Calf raise, each leg | 3 x 15 | 2 x 15 |
| Single-leg glute bridge, each side | 3 x 12 | 2 x 12 |
| Reverse lunge, each side | 3 x 10 | 2 x 10 |
| Side plank, each side | 3 x 40 s | 2 x 40 s |

Good place for it: right after a swim, before the shower. The legs are fresh, the habit is already there.

## Starting below the targets

1. Test each exercise once: the longest clean hold, or the number of clean repetitions.
2. Start the routine at about three quarters of that level, so that three clean sets are possible, on the easier version if the standard one is not clean.
3. On load weeks, add about 5 seconds or 2 repetitions per week. On recovery weeks, use about three quarters of the current level.
4. At the target, stop adding. Move from the easier version to the standard one before adding time.

## Placing it in the week

| Trigger | Routine |
|---|---|
| Evening of the rest day | Core |
| After the midweek swim | Preventive strength, or core in the evening |
| After the second swim | Preventive strength |
| After an easy run or in the evening | Mobility |

Two core sessions a week is the base. Preventive strength comes next, once or twice. Never the day before a test, never before a quality session on the same day.
