# Journal format

Path: `athlete/journal.md`, in the athlete's language. One section per week, newest at the bottom. A line is added at each review and never rewritten.

```markdown
# Journal

## 2026-S49 (30 nov. au 6 déc.) · charge · prévu 5 h 20

| Date | Séance | Prévu | Fait | Effort /10 | Récup. | Lecture | Suite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mar. 1 déc. | 2026-S49-1 seuil | 3x6 min à 5'15/km, 40 min | 41 min, blocs 5'08, 5'14, 5'21 | 8 | en forme | départ trop rapide, blocs en baisse | rien ne change, on reste à 3x6 |
| mer. 2 déc. | 2026-S49-2 natation | 5x100 m à 2'45, 900 m | 900 m, 100 m en 2'44 à 2'47, 24 cycles | 4 | moyen | régulier | rien ne change |

Bilan de la semaine : fait 5 h 10 sur 5 h 20 (97 %). Natation 0 h 55, vélo 2 h 45, course 1 h 30. Séances manquées : aucune. Garde-fous : aucun. Semaine prochaine : décharge, comme prévu.
```

Columns:

- **Date** and **session**: the session id from the week file, and a word for the session.
- **Planned**: the key instruction and the duration.
- **Done**: the facts, in numbers. For a missed session: "missed", with the reason in the athlete's words.
- **Effort out of 10**: as the athlete gives it.
- **Recovery**: that morning's signal, in the athlete's own three levels. Empty if not given.
- **Reading**: the point of the review in a few words. Pain is written here as facts: where, when, at what effort.
- **What comes next**: "nothing changes", or the change and the skill that makes it.

The weekly line: hours done against planned and the percentage, hours by sport, missed sessions, guardrails that fired, what changes next week.

A session kept from several recordings is written once. No medical data in the journal.
