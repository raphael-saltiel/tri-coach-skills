# Guardrail cases, as seen from the review

The athlete is fictional. Each case gives what the journal shows, then the "what comes next" part of the review.

## A missed session

Journal: Tuesday's quality run is marked "missed, late meeting".
Next: "It is not made up. Tomorrow is the swim, as written. The week will end near 4 h 40 instead of 5 h 20. Once, that is nothing."

## More than 10 percent over

Journal on Saturday evening: 5 h 10 done of 5 h 20 planned, Sunday's long run still to come.
Next: "With tomorrow's hour you would end at 6 h 10, 16 percent over. We stop adding: tomorrow is 30 easy minutes or rest. Next week is written from the plan, not from this week."

## Two weeks under 85 percent

Journal: 80 percent, then 77 percent.
Next: "Two weeks under 85 percent: the plan is drifting, I will not write a higher week. Both weeks lost the Thursday trainer session. What is in the way on Thursdays? Once we know, tri-plan rebuilds the ramp from what you really hold."

## Pain on two sessions in a row

Journal: "outside of the right knee from minute 20, at endurance effort" on Tuesday's run, and the same on Thursday's.
Next: "Second run in a row with the same pain. Running stops for the rest of this week. The swims stay. The ride stays only if the knee is silent on the bike: if it speaks, you stop. Nothing is made up. See a doctor or a physiotherapist before running again. I am not telling you what it is: I have noted where, when and at what effort."

## "I could have done more"

Journal: effort 6 out of 10 on a threshold session, with "easy, I could have done a fourth block". First time this is written.
Next: "Noted. Nothing is added: tendons adapt more slowly than the lungs. If the journal says the same for three weeks in a row, you move one step up."

Journal three weeks later: the same remark for the third week running.
Next: "Third week in a row. Next week moves one step: 3x8 min instead of 3x6. tri-semaine rewrites the session."

## Red recovery signal

Journal: recovery "flat" on Thursday morning, trainer quality session planned in the evening.
Next: "Flat this morning: tonight is 30 minutes of easy spinning, or rest. Tomorrow stays as written. The quality session is not moved."

## A threshold changed

Journal: retest at the end of the recovery week, threshold pace 8 seconds per km faster, verdict "ahead".
Next: "New threshold pace. tri-tests recomputes the zones, then tri-semaine rewrites every coming session that quotes the old pace. Until that is done, the numbers in your calendar are wrong."

## A test moved

Journal: the run test planned on Sunday did not happen, travel.
Next: "The test moves to Wednesday. I reread the fifteen days around it: Tuesday's quality run becomes easy (day before the test) and Thursday's session quoted 'your new threshold pace', so it must be rewritten. tri-semaine does both."

## A group ride

Data: 2 h 30 ride. First 50 minutes alone, then 1 h 10 with a slower friend, then 30 minutes alone.
Reading: "I judge the two solo parts: 80 minutes, under the ceiling except four minutes on the first climb. The 1 h 10 with your friend counts in the hours as easy riding and is not judged."

## Two recordings of the same ride

Data: a ride from the bike computer (2 h 02, 45 km) and an automatic activity from a recovery band at the same start time (2 h 05, no distance).
Reading: "Same ride, recorded twice. I keep the bike computer and ignore the band. The week counts 2 h 02, not 4 h 07."

## An easy session done too fast

Data: endurance swim of 1200 m planned at the easy pace, done 12 seconds per 100 m faster, at the pace of the repeats.
Reading: "That was a hard session wearing the label of an easy one. The plan counts 30 minutes, your body counted more. If Thursday feels heavy, this is why."
