# Equipment for a first triathlon

Three piles. Prices and models change: give none from memory.

## Needed to finish

| Sport | Item | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Swim | Swimsuit or tri suit, goggles | A second pair of goggles on race day. The race usually provides the swim cap. |
| Swim | Wetsuit, only if the race swims in cold open water | It can often be rented. Try it in training before the race, never alone. Check the organiser's rule on wetsuits. |
| Swim | A safety buoy for open-water training | Towed behind the swimmer. Not optional. |
| Bike | A bike in working order | A road bike is ideal, but any roadworthy bike finishes a sprint. Have it checked a week before the race. |
| Bike | A helmet | Compulsory in every race. It must be fastened before you touch the bike. |
| Bike | Bottle and cage, spare tube, tyre levers, pump | And knowing how to change a tube: practise once at home. |
| Run | Running shoes you have already run in | Nothing new on race day. |
| All | A race belt for the bib | Avoids pinning and changing. |

## Comfortable

- A tri suit: one outfit for the three sports, no changing in transition.
- Elastic laces: shoes on in seconds.
- Sunglasses for the bike.
- Anti-chafing balm for the neck under a wetsuit and wherever the long runs showed it is needed.
- A watch with a timer. A heart-rate sensor if the athlete wants level 2.
- A home trainer for winter and dark evenings. One that measures power gives level 3 indoors.

## Can wait

- Aero bars, deep wheels, a triathlon bike.
- A power meter on the bike.
- Clipless pedals and cycling shoes: useful later, not needed to finish.
- A gym membership: the strength work of this pack is done at home.
- Gadgets that measure more than the athlete will read.

## Equipment and the three levels of the pack

| Level | What it takes | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A watch with a timer, or a phone | Sessions steered by effort out of 10 and the talk test. Enough to finish any format. |
| 2 | A heart-rate sensor | A ceiling in beats per minute, easier to respect than a feeling. |
| 3 | A trainer that measures power, a GPS watch | Exact blocks indoors, pace on the run. |

The level is a choice, not a ladder. Outdoors, without a power meter on the bike, rides stay at level 1 or 2 whatever the trainer measures.

## Before buying anything

Ask what the athlete already owns, borrow or rent what is used once (the wetsuit), and wait for the first weeks of training to show what is really missing.
