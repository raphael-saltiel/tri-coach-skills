# Tolerance log

One line per session over 90 minutes, in the athlete's language, in `athlete/nutrition.md`.

```markdown
## Journal de tolérance

| Date | Séance | Pris | Glucides | Bu | Conditions | Estomac | Énergie à la fin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sam. 5 déc. | vélo 2 h + 10 min à pied | 1 gel à 45 min, avec de l'eau | 22 g | 600 ml d'eau | 8 °C, endurance | 0 | normale |
| sam. 12 déc. | vélo 1 h 20 | rien (moins de 90 min) | | 400 ml d'eau | 6 °C | | normale |
| sam. 19 déc. | vélo 2 h | 1 gel à 45 min, 1 gel à 1 h 30 | 44 g | 700 ml d'eau | 5 °C, endurance | 1 : ballonnement après le second gel | bonne |
```

Stomach scale: 0 fine, 1 discomfort, 2 had to slow down, 3 had to stop.

Reading the log:

- Two sessions at 0 on the same step: move one step up.
- A 1: stay on the step, or change the form (drink instead of gel) or the timing (smaller, more often).
- A 2 or a 3: back to the last step that went well, for two sessions.
- Energy "flat" at the end with a calm stomach: the step is probably too low for that duration.

What the log never contains: weight, calories of the day, or any medical measurement. If the athlete wants to track something medical during sessions, that belongs with their care team, not here.
