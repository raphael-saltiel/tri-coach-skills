# Placing the week

## How many sessions, and which

Start from the number of sessions the athlete wants and the slots really free. Defaults of this pack, to adjust with the athlete:

| Sessions a week | Swim | Bike | Run | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | The ride is the long session. A light brick replaces the run on brick weeks. |
| 4 | 1 | 1 | 1 | The fourth goes to the weakest sport. For a weak swimmer it is a swim: technique needs frequency. |
| 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 | Or 2, 1, 2 when the bike is the strong sport. |
| 6 | 2 | 2 | 2 | The standard week: one long and one shorter or quality session per sport. |

Short strength sessions (12 to 20 minutes) come on top and are not counted as sessions or hours.

Hours by sport: as a starting point, the bike takes about half the week, the run about a third, the swim the rest. The long ride and the long run come from the plan. What is left is shared between the other sessions.

## Order of placement

1. The full rest day. It opens the week and carries the week note.
2. The swims, because pool hours are the hardest constraint.
3. The long ride and the long run, on the days with the longest free slots, not on the same day.
4. The quality sessions, with an easy day or a swim before and after.
5. The remaining easy sessions.
6. Strength, on easy days or right after a swim, never the day before a test.

## Rules to check once the week is drawn

- No quality session on two consecutive days.
- No quality session the day before a long session.
- Bike quality and run quality on different days.
- The day before a test: rest or an easy swim.
- Every session has a slot and a place that exist in the profile.
- The total matches the plan's minutes, within five percent, and never above them in a recovery week.

## A standard six-session week

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Full rest. Short strength in the evening. |
| Tuesday | Run, quality (or endurance with strides in a first block or a recovery week). |
| Wednesday | Swim. Short strength in the evening. |
| Thursday | Bike, quality or structured endurance, often on the trainer. |
| Friday | Swim, or an easy run. |
| Saturday | Long ride. On brick weeks, a short run straight after. |
| Sunday | Long run, or swim when the long run moved to another day. |

Move the days to fit the athlete's life. Keep the spacing: quality, easy, quality, easy, long, long.

## Recovery week

Same days, shorter sessions, no intensity. Quality sessions become endurance with 4 to 6 strides of 20 seconds. Strength is shortened. If the plan gives a test, it sits on the last day or the last two days, after two easy days.

## Taper weeks

Keep the rhythm of the week (same days, same sports) and cut the durations as the plan says. Keep a few short blocks at race effort. Add nothing new.
